# Sites of the Year — firsthand study

Source collection: https://www.awwwards.com/websites/sites_of_the_year/

This is a bounded firsthand study of the currently linked destinations, completed on 5 November 2026. The public collection reports 64, but its two
accessible pages currently expose 53 distinct award entries. Every entry's
original destination is recorded in `/tmp/year-all-links.json`. HTTP checks and
creator descriptions are supporting evidence, not evidence of experiencing a
website. Current destinations may differ from their awarded versions.

## Firsthand observations and access results

### Lusion — https://lusion.co/

Opened the original site, watched the live field of blue/black/white interlocking
forms, scrolled, and opened/closed the navigation. The forms occupy a dense,
cropped camera composition rather than sitting as isolated objects beside text.
The translucent navigation panels inherit the material/refraction language.
The quiet editorial shell makes the moving composition more forceful. The
current site carries 2026 copy; it is not proof of the precise 2023 award version.

### Messenger — https://messenger.abeto.co/

Opened the original, entered via Begin, advanced the character's introductory
dialogue, and held a mouse destination to walk through the world. The camera
followed the character into a street/building edge. The globe-to-street arrival,
ink outlines, flat painted palette, lettering, dialogue boxes and clothing form
one authored language. The character has a delivery task; movement exposes the
world instead of decorating a conventional landing page. Only the introduction
and a short movement sequence have been played, not all five deliveries.

### Igloo — https://www.igloo.inc/

Opened the live WebGL world. Scrolled from the luminous, disassembling ice-block
igloo in a continuous frozen terrain to a penguin encased in an irregular ice
specimen, accompanied by small technical labels. The project reveal is a
transformation of the environment/material system, not an independent card
animation. Attempted the project exploration control, but did not confirm a
detail page opening. Sound became enabled during interaction; no assessment by
listening is claimed.

### Don't Board Me — https://dontboardme.com/

Opened the original and scrolled through the huge drawn dog into pink service
and process sections. Condensed red display lettering, sparse line illustration,
the continuing leash, warm flat fields, and oversized crops belong to the same
subject. The subject physically organizes the page, rather than sitting in an
illustration box. No booking or message was sent.

### Lando Norris — https://landonorris.com/

Opened the original, watched the large face/helmet reveal, scrolled into the
dark olive editorial-photo sequence, and opened the full navigation. The
portrait, helmet, sports reference lines, lime accents, serif/sans contrast and
on-track/off-track framing express the driver's identity. Photography and type
carry much of the experience; 3D is not its only quality signal.

### Opal Tadpole — original link redirects to https://op.al/

Opened the award's original product URL. It redirects to the current Opal
Electronics company letter, dated June 1, 2026. The awarded Tadpole experience is
not present at that destination. Do not equate the current letter with the old
award or claim to have interacted with the original product sequence.

### Noomo — https://noomoagency.com/

Opened the current original domain and scrolled into services and testimonials.
A purple jellyfish/glass object and chunky pixel-like forms float across pale
blue editorial typography. Some text layers overlapped in this browser during
transition. Current enterprise/AI copy differs from the award-era description;
this pass is evidence of the current site, not the earlier version.

### Mana Yerba Mate — https://en.manayerbamate.com/

Opened the original domain, dismissed a large seasonal promotional overlay, and

### KPR — https://kprverse.com/

### The Other Side of Truth — https://theothersideoftruth.com/

Opened the original, viewed the enormous animated lettering and blue/yellow
field, switched the Russian Truth control and scrolled. The palette changed to

it is not ornamental. This is a design observation, not fact-checking of its
historical statistics or endorsement of every claim in the page.

### Persepolis Reimagined — https://persepolis.getty.edu/

Followed the original Getty redirect, scrolled through the night-to-day
introduction, clicked Enter and progressed to the Gate of All Nations. The
architectural approach, visitor point of view, atmospheric lighting, restrained
serif captions and chapter structure make the spatial model explanatory. It is
a place to learn through movement, not a decorative building.

### Pangram Pangram — https://pangrampangram.com/

### Inside the Head — https://insidethehead.co/

Opened the original, watched its typed introduction, entered the chapter selector
and opened Age. Abstract colored bands resolved into nested profile silhouettes
around chapter titles. Selecting a chapter yielded a quiet cream reading view,
with a short centered verse, page count and Next control. The expressive identity
steps back when reading becomes the task. No 3D is needed for that distinction.

## Synthesis

In the sequences inspected, the subject supplies a distinct **organizing principle**. That can be an interaction premise, an editorial structure, or the product itself; it is not a bag of effects.
Messenger makes moving through a world the task; Nomadic Tribe makes a gesture create
life; Persepolis makes camera travel teach a place; Orano makes spatial inspection
explain a device; Pangram makes the typography the product and the interface.

Their quality is evident in deliberately composed states and authored transitions
in the sequences inspected, and a visual language that reaches into controls, typography, motion,
lighting and content. Some are deliberately simple and some are highly elaborate.
Neither large text nor 3D is a substitute for that coherence.

For Neighborly, the rejected prototype does not meet this standard. Its technically
working state machine and supplied Blender files are not visual acceptance. The
whole experience needs an original interaction premise and authored sequence; it
must not be repaired by merely polishing the three existing figures.
